## 👨‍🔬 Principal Investigator

**Dr. Ki-Seok Kim (김기석, Ph.D.)**
Head of Advanced Prediction Research Division
National Institute of Toxicological Research (NITR / 국가독성과학연구소)

Dr. Kim leads research on organoid-based platforms for neurotoxicology and alternative animal testing. His lab pioneered the use of human stem cell-derived **brain organoids** for assessing drug-induced neurodevelopmental toxicity.

**Key Publication:**
> *"Chronic Haloperidol Exposure Impairs Neurodevelopment via Notch1 Signaling in Human Stem Cell-Derived Brain Organoids"*
> **Scientific Reports** (Nature Publishing Group), Vol. 15, Article 25945, 2025
> — First demonstration using human brain organoids that antipsychotic drugs impair neurodevelopment via Notch1 signaling pathway disruption.

**Research Areas:**
- Human stem cell-derived brain organoid models
- Notch1 signaling in neurodevelopmental toxicity
- Alternative methods to replace animal models in regulatory toxicology
- CNS drug safety: antipsychotics, antidepressants, anticonvulsants
- Translational toxicology for OECD/ISO standard development
